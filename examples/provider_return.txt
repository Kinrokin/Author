# Chapter 1 — The Orchard Clock

Mara arrived before the bell. The orchard had beaten her there.

Ticking moved through the pear trees in scattered pockets: one branch hurried, another dragged, a third stopped whenever she looked directly at it. Tomas waited beneath the oldest trunk with his fists buried in his coat.

“You touched it.”

“I listened.”

“That wasn’t the question.”

A pear dropped between them and rang against the frost.

Tomas flinched. Mara did not bend for it.
