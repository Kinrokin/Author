# Chapter 1 — The Orchard Clock

Mara reached the orchard before the town bell and found every pear tree ticking.

Not loudly. Not together. Each branch kept its own little time, as if the frost had left a watch inside the wood. Her brother Tomas stood under the oldest tree with both hands in his coat.

“You touched it,” she said.

“I listened.”

“That is not what I asked.”

A pear fell between them. It struck the frozen ground and chimed once.

Neither of them moved to pick it up.
